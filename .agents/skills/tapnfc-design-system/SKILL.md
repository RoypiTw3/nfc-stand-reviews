# TapNFC Design System & Brand Identity

## Core Aesthetics
- **Style**: High-end Swiss Editorial & Minimal Luxury Tech.
- **Palette**:
  - Pure Dark: `#070809` (Background)
  - Dark Surface / Elevation: `#0f1013`, `#14161b`
  - High Contrast Primary: `#ffffff` (Headings)
  - Muted Editorial Text: `#8a919e`, `#636b77`
  - Accents: Subtle emerald badge `#10b981`, Google Gold `#f59e0b`, Google Blue `#4285f4`.
- **Typography**: Clean grotesque sans-serif (`Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, system-ui) with tight tracking (`letter-spacing: -0.02em` to `-0.04em`) on large display titles.

## Conversion & Frictionless Funnel Rules
- **WhatsApp Integration**:
  - Always route actions to `+57 315 185 6554` (`https://wa.me/573151856554`).
  - Pre-fill clean, natural Spanish messages without confusing emoji characters or broken encoding.
  - Integrate qualification questions (e.g. Google Business Profile status).

## Hardware & Media Principles
- Video assets must use seamless native mask blends (`mask-image: radial-gradient(...)`) rather than harsh borders or fake device frames unless explicitly requested.
- Hardware collections must highlight physical premium materials (Matte Acrylic, European Solid Oak, Anodized Titanium & NFC Cards).
