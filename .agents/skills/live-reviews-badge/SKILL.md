# Live Reviews & Social Proof Badge Skill

## Core Directives
1. **Visual Social Proof Hierarchy**:
   - Prominent rating display (e.g. `★ 4.9 / 5.0`).
   - Verified local business badge / Google Maps sync icon.
   - Clean typographic counter (e.g. `+1.200 reseñas de 5 estrellas conseguidas`).
2. **Animation & Interaction**:
   - Subtle pulse or shine on the Google star badge.
   - Smooth count-up easing when the badge scrolls into view.
3. **Conversion Placement**:
   - Place directly beneath primary hero CTA or above product collection to minimize buyer hesitation.
