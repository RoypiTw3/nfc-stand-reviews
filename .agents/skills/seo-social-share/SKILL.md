# SEO & Social Share Optimization Skill

## Core Directives

### 1. Open Graph & Twitter Cards
- Always specify high-resolution 1200x630px social previews (`og:image`, `twitter:image`).
- Set `og:type` to `website` or `product`.
- Set accurate `og:site_name`, `og:title`, and `og:description`.
- Use `summary_large_image` for Twitter Cards.

### 2. Structured Data (Schema.org / JSON-LD)
- Embed structured microdata in `<script type="application/ld+json">`.
- Use `Product`, `LocalBusiness`, or `Service` schemas with proper pricing currency (`COP`), reviews rating aggregator, and brand specifications.

### 3. Canonical & Meta Tags
- Always specify `<link rel="canonical" href="...">`.
- Include standard mobile viewport configuration: `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`.
- Provide meaningful `meta name="description"` (between 140 and 160 characters).
- Include `theme-color` matching the primary background (`#070809`).
